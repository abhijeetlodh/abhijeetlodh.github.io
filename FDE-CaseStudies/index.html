CASE STUDY 01

Secure Internal Document Search for a Bank

A compliance-first RAG platform for policies, procedures, product documents, risk memos, and customer-service knowledge.

Primary Objective
Reduce time searching internal procedures & product rules
Best Buyer
Relationship Managers, Compliance Officers, Operations
First Release
One high-value domain e.g. KYC onboarding
Decision Posture
Assistive AI — human review mandatory

Business Context

A mid-sized bank has thousands of internal documents across SharePoint, Confluence, network drives, policy repositories and archived attachments. Employees waste time searching, answers differ by department, and sensitive documents can't be exposed broadly. The bank wants an AI assistant that answers with citations while respecting each employee's access rights.

Business Goals

Reduce time spent searching internal procedures and product rules.

Improve answer consistency across branch, risk, compliance & support teams.

Enforce document- and chunk-level access control.

Provide auditable citations and retrieval traces for compliance.

Detect outdated, conflicting and unapproved documents.

Reference Architecture

ComponentRole in the System
Source connectorsSharePoint, Confluence, file shares, policy repos, uploaded PDFs.
Ingestion servicePulls approved docs, OCRs scans, checksums, classifies sensitivity, stores normalized text.
Metadata layerOwner, approval status, dept, confidentiality, effective/expiry date, ACL tags.
Vector + keyword indexEmbeddings for semantic recall + BM25 for exact form/policy numbers.
Retrieval orchestratorKeyword + vector search, re-ranking, permission filtering, citations.
LLM gatewayCentralized calls, prompt templates, safety filters, redaction, audit logging.
Monitoring & auditLogs query, retrieved IDs, answer hash, user ID, access decision, feedback.

Security & Governance

SSO + short-lived tokens, MFA for privileged users.

Permission filtering before the LLM sees content — unauthorized chunks never enter the prompt.

Encryption in transit & at rest for docs, text, embeddings, logs.

Prompt-injection defense: treat retrieved content as untrusted reference.

Full audit trail; no training on bank data by default.

KPIs

MetricHow to Measure
Search success rate% sessions where user accepts answer or opens cited source.
Answer groundedness% answer claims supported by citations during audit sampling.
Permission leakageUnauthorized exposures — target zero.
Stale-source rateShare of answers citing expired/unapproved docs.

Key Risks & Mitigations

RiskMitigation
Unauthorized accessPre-generation permission filtering, policy-as-code, automated access tests.
Hallucinated adviceStrict prompts, cite every claim, show uncertainty, source-open links.
Outdated documentsBlock expired docs by default; alert owners before expiry.
Conflicting rulesDetect conflicts & show warnings instead of forcing one answer.
CASE STUDY 02

AI CV Matching Platform for Recruiting

A human-in-the-loop matching assistant that compares CVs against job descriptions, explains fit, and improves recruiter productivity — without making final hiring decisions.

Primary Objective
Reduce repetitive CV screening time
Best Buyer
Recruiters, Hiring Managers, Talent Ops
First Release
One job family e.g. Java backend / FDE roles
Decision Posture
Assistive AI — human review mandatory

Business Goals

Reduce repetitive CV screening time for recruiters.

Produce structured candidate summaries mapped to requirements.

Avoid automated rejection or ranking on protected attributes.

Create a searchable talent pool for future roles.

Scope Boundaries

In ScopeOut of Scope
CV parsing & structured profile extractionAutomatic rejection of candidates
Job-description requirement extractionInferring protected characteristics
Candidate-to-role match explanationScraping candidate data without consent
Recruiter review workflow & talent searchReplacing recruiter / hiring-manager judgment

Data Model

EntityImportant Fields
CandidateProfilecandidate_id, skills, experience_years, education, certifications, parsed_sections
JobRequirementjob_id, must_have, nice_to_have, seniority, confirmed_by_recruiter
MatchResultcandidate_id, job_id, fit_label, evidence, gaps, suggested_questions
RecruiterDecisiondecision_id, decision, reason, reviewer_id, override_notes

KPIs & Risks

Metric / RiskDetail
Screening time/candidateRecruiter minutes saved per CV.
Evidence coverage% match explanations with explicit CV evidence.
⚠ Bias & unfair screeningRemove protected attributes, require human review, audit outputs.
⚠ Over-reliance on scoreUse fit labels + evidence summaries, not a single ranking.
CASE STUDY 03

Support Ticket Triage Assistant

An AI workflow that classifies tickets, detects urgency, suggests owners, drafts replies, and escalates risky cases to humans.

Primary Objective
Reduce first-response time & manual routing
Best Buyer
Support Agents, Managers, Engineering teams
First Release
One helpdesk tool + one or two product queues
Decision Posture
Assistive AI — human review mandatory

Reference Architecture

ComponentRole
Input connectorsZendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Jira SM, inboxes, webhooks.
Classifier pipelineCategory, severity, sentiment, urgency, duplicate probability, escalation keywords.
Knowledge retrievalProduct docs, runbooks, release notes, known issues, resolved tickets.
Recommendation engineSuggests queue, owner, priority, tags, draft response with citations.
Feedback loopCaptures agent edits, misroutes, post-resolution labels.

Security & Governance

Redact API keys, passwords, tokens & PII before LLM calls.

Tenant isolation — one customer's content can't appear in another's answer.

Human approval before external replies in legal/billing/security/outage cases.

Allowlisted automation actions to prevent unsafe updates.

KPIs

MetricHow to Measure
First response timeMedian time to first meaningful response.
Routing accuracy% tickets assigned to correct queue on first attempt.
SLA breach rate% tickets missing SLA deadlines.
Agent acceptance% AI-suggested fields/drafts accepted.
CASE STUDY 04

Insurance Claim Document Intelligence

An AI document-processing workflow that extracts claim facts, validates forms, summarizes evidence, and assists adjusters.

Primary Objective
Reduce manual document review for adjusters
Best Buyer
Adjusters, Intake teams, SIU
First Release
One claim type e.g. auto glass / small property
Decision Posture
Assistive AI — human review mandatory

Reference Architecture

ComponentRole
Document intakePortals, email, mobile app, brokers, call-center, third-party assessors.
OCR & parsingText, tables, stamps, dates, signatures, handwriting, key-value pairs.
Extraction pipelineClaimant, policy #, date of loss, invoice amount, provider, location.
Evidence storeLinks every field to source doc, page, bounding area & confidence.
Rules & analyticsCoverage checks, duplicate checks, fraud-signal scoring.

Data Model

EntityImportant Fields
Claimclaim_id, policy_id, claim_type, date_of_loss, status, total_claimed_amount
ClaimDocumentdocument_id, type, file_uri, ocr_text_uri, classification_confidence
ExtractedFactfield_name, value, confidence, page, bounding_box, reviewer_status

Key Risks & Mitigations

RiskMitigation
Incorrect extractionShow confidence + evidence, require review for critical fields.
Overstated fraud flagsUse "requires review" language; keep investigators human-led.
Poor OCR on scansImage-quality checks, fallback manual review, re-upload prompts.
Coverage misinterpretationRules engine + policy citations; LLM never makes final coverage call.
CASE STUDY 05

CRM Enrichment & Sales Workflow Automation

An AI-assisted sales operations platform that cleans CRM records, enriches accounts, recommends next actions, and automates routine follow-ups.

Primary Objective
Improve CRM data quality, reduce duplicates
Best Buyer
Sales Reps, Sales Managers, RevOps
First Release
One CRM + one segment e.g. mid-market SaaS
Decision Posture
Assistive AI — human review mandatory

Reference Architecture

ComponentRole
CRM connectorSalesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics, Pipedrive — accounts, contacts, opportunities.
Data-quality engineMissing fields, duplicates, stale opps, invalid emails, inconsistent taxonomy.
Entity resolutionMatches duplicates via domain, name, email, phone, fuzzy similarity.
AI account briefSummarizes context, activity, pain points, stakeholders, talking points.
Governance layerData provenance, source freshness, user approvals, field update history.

KPIs & Risks

Metric / RiskDetail
CRM completeness% accounts with required fields populated.
Duplicate reductionNumber of duplicate accounts/contacts resolved.
⚠ Bad enrichment dataTrack provenance & confidence; let users reject updates.
⚠ CRM pollutionNever auto-write low-confidence data; use staging + approval queues.

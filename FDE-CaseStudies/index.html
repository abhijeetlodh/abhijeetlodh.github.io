About This Document

Each case study is a practical implementation blueprint — use it to pitch a client, prepare a portfolio project, design a SaaS MVP, or discuss enterprise AI architecture in interviews. It distills 5 years of personal field experience into curated, reusable assets.

Design Philosophy
Human-in-the-loop, governance, ROI, source-backed, auditability, safe automation
Primary Buyers
Banks, Recruiting, SaaS Support, Insurance, RevOps, Fraud, Consulting
Positioning Note
Assistive AI. For high-impact decisions, humans stay accountable — model never decides alone.

Reusable Case Study Structure

Business context — the organizational pain & why AI matters.

Users & workflows — who uses it, before vs after AI.

Architecture — sources, ingestion, orchestration, model, UI, monitoring, governance.

Security & compliance — access control, privacy, audit, human review.

MVP plan, KPIs & Risks — launch lean, prove value, mitigate failure.

Appendix A: Universal Delivery Checklist

AreaVerify
Data inventoryEvery source, owner, sensitivity, update frequency, retention rule.
Access controlPermission filtering before generation, not only in UI.
Evidence & citationsEvery material answer links to a source/evidence object.
Human reviewHumans responsible for high-impact/regulated decisions.
Audit trailLog query, sources, model & prompt version, output, user action.
EvaluationTest groundedness, extraction, leakage, latency, cost.
FallbackDefine behavior when evidence is missing/conflicting/outdated.
MonitoringUsage, quality, errors, drift, cost, latency, security events.
Legal reviewPrivacy, retention, consent, model terms, decision boundaries.

Appendix B: Reusable Discovery Questions

Which workflow is slow, expensive, inconsistent, or compliance-heavy?

Who will trust the assistant, and who may resist it?

What systems hold the source data, and who owns them?

What should AI only recommend vs perform automatically?

Consequences of a wrong answer, extraction, routing, or leak?

Which metrics prove value in 30/60/90 days?

What records are outdated, duplicated, confidential, or restricted?

What approval, audit & escalation workflows already exist?

Which integrations are mandatory for adoption?

What is the smallest pilot proving value without risk?

Closing Note

The strongest enterprise AI opportunities are not generic chatbots. They are governed assistants embedded into real workflows, connected to enterprise data, designed with measurable KPIs, and constrained by human review where decisions are sensitive.
